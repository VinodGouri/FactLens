About the project

Base paper

Anusha, K. M. Shenoy, S. N. Pai, “RAG-Enhanced DistilBERT Model for Contextual Understanding of Polysemy in Digital News Titles,” IEEE Access, vol. 14, pp. 90425–90443, 2026. doi: 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3702098

Dataset

10,240 short political statements (LIAR / PolitiFact training split), binarised into Real (true, mostly-true, half-true) and Fake (false, barely-true, pants-on-fire). Stratified 70/15/15 split.

Proposed system
  • RAG retriever: TF-IDF search over training-split fact-checked claims (with verdicts).
  • Polysemy module: WordNet glosses of polysemous words, disambiguated with Lesk using the retrieved evidence.
  • Models: TF-IDF + LR, RoBERTa-base, RAG-RoBERTa-base, RAG-DistilRoBERTa-KD (paper's RAG-DistilBERT recipe), RAG-XLM-R-base, and a weighted soft-voting ensemble.
